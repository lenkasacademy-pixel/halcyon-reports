How this was built. Campaign-level daily figures were pulled per account from the Meta Ads API and summed. For both accounts, and on every day in both reporting windows, the campaign sums match the account’s own daily spend and impressions exactly — nothing is missing or double-counted.

  • Calls are Meta’s Phone calls placed (click_to_call_native_call_placed), the only call metric Meta exposes. It counts taps that opened the dialler, not conversations answered at the clinic, so the clinic’s own log will read lower. One archived September campaign (4 Numbers) is labelled a lead campaign by Meta, which leaves its results blank; its calls are its spend divided by Meta’s cost per call placed, and every day divides to a whole number.
  • Cost per call divides only the spend of campaigns optimising for calls. Blending in the website-lead budget would overstate what a call costs.
  • 20s+ and 60s+ are derived, not reported. Meta exposes no count for them — only an average cost per connect — so each figure is that campaign's spend divided by its click_to_call_native_20s_call_connect (or 60s) cost. Every division lands exactly on a whole number, which is the check that the derivation is sound. They are derived per campaign per period, so they appear in the funnel and the campaign table but not in the day table.
  • September stops at the 28th. Meta keeps revising the most recent ~48 hours, so 29 September is a live part-day and is excluded rather than shown as a collapse in spend. It will settle into the next refresh.
  • Reach in the day tables is summed per day and double-counts people across days; the de-duplicated figure for each window is on the Reach tile above.
  • A few paise of call spend fall in an Unknown age band and are omitted from the age table only.
  • All figures are ex-GST. Meta bills 18% GST on top in India.
